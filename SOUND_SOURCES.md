# Animal recording sources

22 animals have local audio files: 20 animal recordings (including rabbit chewing) and 2 pre-existing cinematic dinosaur effects. **8 animals intentionally remain silent.** None use the removed synthesized animal recipes or a substitute from another animal. The original synthesized celebration jingle is retained.

All files are mono MP3, 44.1 kHz, 64 kbps. Total: **259,235 bytes (253.2 KiB)**. Longest encoded file: **2.168163 s**; no file exceeds 3 seconds. Loudness processing targets -24 LUFS and a -6 dBTP ceiling, followed by quiet in-app gain. MP3 encoder delay is included in the durations below.

Recordings are included in the complete offline precache. Attribution is also available offline in the app footer under **Zvuky a licence**. The licenses below apply to the individual recordings and their modified MP3 derivatives, independently of the application code. CC BY-SA derivatives (giraffe and dolphin) remain CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Inspection and limits

Checked on 2026-10-19. Source descriptions and licenses were inspected; every selected file was downloaded, decoded, and checked for duration, waveform, loudness and clipping. Short segments were selected using the decoded waveforms. **These are not claimed to have received a human listening review.** A parent should audition them on the iPad before use with a child. The sources identify the animal recordings; dinosaur voices are fictional prerecorded effects, not authentic recordings of extinct animals. Rabbit audio is chewing, not a vocalization. The monkey/Opice entry is specifically a gibbon call.

## Source priority and exclusions

Freesound was searched first (CC0 preferred). Actual field recordings were distinguished from human imitations, toys and synthesized effects. Numerous page/preview requests returned HTTP 403 or 429; these restrictions were not bypassed. Examples include [Dog bark 2](https://freesound.org/people/Sadiquecat/sounds/850823/), [Cat Meow](https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/813119/), and [hamster.mp3](https://freesound.org/people/Olivaque/sounds/181426/). The public previews could not be downloaded.

Pixabay was checked next; downloads returned HTTP 403. Its [Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/) also restricts standalone distribution; it was **not treated as CC0** and no Pixabay assets are included. Third-party sources below were used only after checking an explicit license permitting redistribution. SoundBible clips labelled Personal Use Only (including some bear, horse, dolphin and zebra recordings) and Sampling Plus 1.0 were rejected. No rights were inferred from a "royalty-free" heading or a filename.

OpenGameArt's penguin archive includes the original Freesound attribution in its README (Bidone, https://freesound.org/s/66150/). Giraffe's archive README explicitly states CC BY-SA 4.0 and credits the researchers; that license is retained despite the page header saying CC BY 4.0. Dolphin's page offers CC BY-SA 4.0/3.0; CC BY-SA 4.0 is retained, with credit to ZooSpensefull and AntumDeluge. Zebra's page offers CC BY 3.0 or later; CC BY 3.0 is selected.

## Intentionally silent animals

These IDs map explicitly to `null`. They still hatch normally and receive the celebration jingle when sound is enabled.

- **Křeček (`hamster`):** Freesound hamster.mp3 by Olivaque is CC0, but its public preview returned HTTP 403. Other hits were nest/cage noises or toys. No suitable downloadable replacement was verified on Pixabay, SoundBible or OpenGameArt.
- **Liška (`fox`):** Freesound real fox-call candidates were inaccessible; many are screams. SoundBible alternatives are wolves/coyotes, so were rejected. No suitable downloadable, correctly identified replacement was verified.
- **Panda (`panda`):** Freesound/Pixabay/OpenGameArt searches returned music, toys, human speech or red pandas rather than a verified giant-panda recording. No suitable recording obtained.
- **Želva (`turtle`):** Freesound/Pixabay/other-library results were turtle doves, transitions, imitations or unsuitable material; no suitable turtle recording obtained.
- **Chobotnice (`octopus`):** Search results were music, ride recordings or foley with unverified downloads. No suitable natural-sounding, licensed recording obtained.
- **Krab (`crab`):** Freesound candidates are foley/imitations and were inaccessible; Pixabay downloads were blocked; other-library searches did not yield a suitable verified recording.
- **Triceratops (`triceratops`):** Freesound creature/dinosaur candidates were inaccessible. Pixabay downloads were blocked. No additional distinct, gentle prerecorded effect with verified redistribution rights was obtained; existing dinosaur files are not reused.
- **Stegosaurus (`stegosaurus`):** Freesound creature/dinosaur candidates were inaccessible. Pixabay downloads were blocked. No additional distinct, gentle prerecorded effect with verified redistribution rights was obtained; existing dinosaur files are not reused.

## Technical provenance

[`assets/sounds/animals/sources.json`](assets/sounds/animals/sources.json) records direct download URLs, source filenames and SHA-256 digests, final asset hashes, segment boundaries, processing and exact encoded durations. Original source files are not duplicated in the deployed app. `scripts/validate-animal-audio.py` checks the committed MP3s using FFmpeg/ffprobe; browser tests independently decode them in Chromium and WebKit, including offline.
